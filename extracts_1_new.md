### W03 · Heloise d'Argenteuil: Heloise's first letter to Abelard
*c. 1132 · The Paraclete convent, Champagne, France · female · Europe (France)*

**Tags:** Mad › Hurt / Distant (primary); Sad › Lonely / Isolated (primary); Peaceful › Intimate / Responsive (secondary)

**Source:** The Love Letters of Abelard and Heloise (Seymour, 1903; reprinting the 1722 English version), Project Gutenberg #40227, Heloise's first letter to Abelard · **Type:** verbatim (public domain)

> A consolatory letter of yours to a friend happened some days since to fall into my hands; my knowledge of the writing and my love of the hand gave me the curiosity to open it. [...] What reflections did I not make! I began to consider the whole afresh, and perceived myself pressed with the same weight of grief as when we first began to be miserable. >

**Note:** This 18th-century English version is a loose rendering (via French), not a close translation of the Latin. Say 'from an 18th-century English version' in the app.

**Tags:** Joyful › Hopeful / Optimistic (primary); Peaceful › Nurturing / Thankful (secondary); Sad › Depressed / Inferior (secondary)

### W13 · Tao Yuanming (Tao Qian): 'Return Home!' (Gui qu lai xi ci), with preface
*405 CE · Pengze county, Jiangxi, China · male · East Asia (China)*

**Tags:** Sad › Tired / Sleepy (primary); Sad › Guilty / Remorseful (secondary); Peaceful › Content / Relaxed (secondary)  
**Intensity:** 1

**Source:** Translated for this project from the Chinese original (歸去來兮辭并序, 405 CE), text via Chinese Wikisource, Prose preface, and the opening lines of the rhapsody · **Type:** our translation from the original

> My family was poor, and farming could not feed us. The house was full of small children and there was no grain in the jar. [...] So when a post came up in Pengze, a hundred li from home, I took it. But within a few days I felt a longing to go home. Why? My nature is what it is; it cannot be forced into another shape. Hunger and cold are real enough, but going against myself made me sick in body and mind. [...]
>
> Return home! The fields and garden will soon be overgrown; why not go back?
> I have made my mind the servant of my body; why be miserable and grieve alone?
> I see now the past can't be put right, but I know the future can still be reached.
> I have not gone so far down the wrong road; I can see today is right and yesterday was wrong.

**Original:** 余家貧，耕植不足以自給。幼稚盈室，缾無儲粟……及少日，眷然有歸歟之情。何則？質性自然，非矯勵所得。飢凍雖切，違己交病。……歸去來兮，田園將蕪胡不歸？既自以心為形役，奚惆悵而獨悲！悟已往之不諫，知來者之可追；實迷途其未遠，覺今是而昨非。
